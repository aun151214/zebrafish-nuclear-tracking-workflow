# Mastodon track/component continuity

This module summarises threshold-6 Mastodon recovery as temporal units rather
than only independent spots and links.

- The ten-frame profile excludes cross-track division links and analyses the
  reviewed track segments inside Mastodon frames 0–9.
- The full-duration profile preserves the legacy source-component assignment
  for adjacent reviewed links.
- Statuses are: fully recovered; all spots matched with at least one link
  break; partially recovered; and not recovered.
- Optional reconstruction of one-to-one iTEC tracklets reports whether a
  reviewed segment maps to zero, one, or multiple iTEC tracklets.

The sparse reference measures recovery of reviewed annotations, not global
segmentation precision or a complete identity-switch rate.
